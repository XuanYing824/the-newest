Community & Accessibility

BlindPath is designed to support a community-led approach to more accessible townships.

How Problem Resolution Works

  • Active reports place a Red Cross (✖) on the community map.
  • Passersby can inspect the location and vote whether the issue is resolved.
  • Once verified resolved, the Red Cross marker and photo are automatically cleared.
